Free / Accessible AI Tools for Everyday People

Generative AI & Productivity

ChatGPT (Free Tier) — GPT-4o-mini, millions using for dispute letters, form completion, translating bureaucratic jargon. chat.openai.com

Google Gemini (Free) — 2M context, good for summarizing long documents (leases, insurance policies). gemini.google.com

Microsoft Copilot (Free) — Embedded in Windows/Bing. Used by tenants for repair-request emails, workers for labor rights. copilot.microsoft.com

Claude (Free) — 200K context, dumping entire PDFs and asking targeted questions. claude.ai

Hugging Face Transformers / Spaces — Open-source model hub. Run Llama 3, Mistral locally. Privacy-critical for sensitive groups. huggingface.co

Practical / Task-Specific

DoNotPay — "Robot lawyer" for parking tickets, small claims, tenant rights. Claims $4M+ in overturned fines. Free/low-cost tiers. donotpay.com

JustFix — Free open-source tools for NYC tenants. Build habitability cases, request repairs. justfix.org

Upsolve — Free nonprofit Chapter 7 bankruptcy filing. Over $500M in debt relieved. upsolve.org

Spot — AI chatbot for workplace harassment reporting. Generates legally admissible documentation.

LawDroid — No-code legal-aid chatbots. Auto-generates court answer files. Free for 501(c)(3)s.

AI in Education, Healthcare & Legal Aid

Education

Khanmigo — GPT-4 tutor free to low-income districts. 1.5x engagement increase in pilots. khanacademy.org/khanmigo

AI4ALL — Nonprofit bringing AI literacy to underrepresented high schoolers. ai-4-all.org

Grow with Google / AI Essentials — Free certificate (under 10 hours). In Spanish. Career-changers without degrees. grow.google/ai-essentials

Healthcare

Buoy Health — Free AI triage designed for low-literacy users. Directs uninsured to sliding-scale clinics. buoyhealth.com

Ada Health — 10-language symptom assessment. Used heavily in Global South. ada.com

Woebot — Free CBT chatbot. JAMA-published RCT showed depression reduction in postpartum women. woebothealth.com

Legal Aid

LSC AI Grants (2024) — Funded 10 legal-aid nonprofits for eviction defense, immigrant rights, benefits appeals. Target: close "justice gap" where 92% of low-income Americans get no civil legal help.

Paladin — Pro-bono attorney matching with AI. 30+ states. joinpaladin.com

Data-as-Power Movements

Housing

Housing Data Coalition (NYC) — Scraping court records into structured databases. Tenants prove "pattern of neglect." housingdatanyc.org

Who Owns What (JustFix) — Open database mapping corporate landlords. LLC-network analysis to surface shell companies. whoownswhat.justfix.org

Rentervention (Illinois) — Chatbot + case-management. Auto-fills Chicago tenant ordinance notices.

Workers & Wages

Wage Theft Calculator (EPI) — Simple tool calculating stolen wages. $50B/year in U.S. wage theft. epi.org

ShedLight — Workers upload pay stubs; AI extracts violations and generates demand letters.

Immigration

Immigration Voice + AI Data Team — Scraped USCIS data to identify processing outliers. 10,000+ cases for Writ of Mandamus filing. Win rate >90%.

Policing

Open Policing Project (Stanford) — 200M+ traffic-stop records. Found Black drivers 20% more likely to be searched. Used in class-action litigation. openpolicing.stanford.edu

Citizen Browser (The Markup) — Paid panelists scraping Facebook algorithmic recommendations. Proved algorithm pushed inflammatory content more to Black users. themarkup.org/citizen-browser

Real Case Studies

Housing · New York City

The "Worst Evictors" Database

NYC's ANHD built eviction/violation databases. City Council passed disclosure law. Buildings converted to community land trusts. Sources: ANHD Annual Report 2023; The City (NYC).

Healthcare · Zambia

AI TB Screening With Zero On-Site Radiologists

Zebra Medical Vision deployed AI chest-X-ray in rural clinics with zero radiologists. 3x TB case detection. Treatment initiation from 3 weeks to 48 hours. Source: The Lancet Digital Health, 2021.

Labor · California

Amazon Workers Logged Algorithmic Firings

Workers logged "Time Off Task" firings. Data showed firings correlated with understaffed shifts, not laziness. California OSHA investigations; Warehouse Worker Protection Act signed (2022).

Education · Newark, NJ

Khanmigo in Title I Middle Schools

Title I middle schools pilot. 20% increase in math-assignment completion.

Immigration · National

FOIA + AI = Forced Adjudication

Immigration Voice scraped USCIS case-status data and court filing records. NLP identified processing delays exceeding statutory norms. 10,000+ cases flagged as eligible for Writ of Mandamus. Win rate >90%.
