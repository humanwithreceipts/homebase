# AI Empowerment Research
## Compiled for humanwithreceipts.com | 2026-07-05

## 1. FREE / ACCESSIBLE AI TOOLS FOR EVERYDAY PEOPLE

### Generative AI & Productivity
- **ChatGPT (Free Tier)** — GPT-4o-mini, millions using for dispute letters, form completion, translating bureaucratic jargon. https://chat.openai.com
- **Google Gemini (Free)** — 2M context, good for summarizing long documents (leases, insurance policies). https://gemini.google.com
- **Microsoft Copilot (Free)** — Embedded in Windows/Bing. Used by tenants for repair-request emails, workers for labor rights. https://copilot.microsoft.com
- **Claude (Free)** — 200K context, dumping entire PDFs and asking targeted questions. https://claude.ai
- **Hugging Face Transformers / Spaces** — Open-source model hub. Run Llama 3, Mistral locally. Privacy-critical for sensitive groups. https://huggingface.co

### Practical / Task-Specific Free Tools
- **DoNotPay** — "Robot lawyer" for parking tickets, small claims, tenant rights. Claims $4M+ in overturned fines. Free/low-cost tiers.
- **JustFix** — Free open-source tools for NYC tenants. Build habitability cases, request repairs.
- **Upsolve** — Free nonprofit Chapter 7 bankruptcy filing. Over $500M in debt relieved.
- **Spot** — AI chatbot for workplace harassment reporting. Generates legally admissible documentation.
- **LawDroid** — No-code legal-aid chatbots. Auto-generates court answer files. Free for 501(c)(3)s.

## 2. AI IN EDUCATION, HEALTHCARE & LEGAL AID

### Education
- **Khanmigo** — GPT-4 tutor free to low-income districts. 1.5x engagement increase in pilots.
- **AI4ALL** — Nonprofit bringing AI literacy to underrepresented high schoolers. https://ai-4-all.org
- **Grow with Google / AI Essentials** — Free certificate (under 10 hours). In Spanish. Career-changers without degrees. https://grow.google/ai-essentials

### Healthcare
- **Buoy Health (Free)** — AI triage designed for low-literacy users. Directs uninsured to sliding-scale clinics.
- **Ada Health (Free)** — 10-language symptom assessment. Used heavily in Global South.
- **Woebot** — Free CBT chatbot. JAMA-published RCT showed depression reduction in postpartum women.

### Legal Aid
- **LSC AI Grants (2024)** — Funded 10 legal-aid nonprofits for eviction defense, immigrant rights, benefits appeals. Target: close "justice gap" where 92% of low-income Americans get no civil legal help.
- **Paladin** — Pro-bono attorney matching with AI. 30+ states. https://joinpaladin.com

**Key Statistic:** "For every low-income person who receives legal aid, another 92% of eligible individuals go unassisted." — Legal Services Corporation, Justice Gap Report 2022.

## 3. DATA-AS-POWER MOVEMENTS

### Housing
- **Housing Data Coalition (NYC)** — Scraping court records into structured databases. Tenants prove "pattern of neglect."
- **Who Owns What (JustFix)** — Open database mapping corporate landlords. LLC-network analysis to surface shell companies.
- **Rentervention (Illinois)** — Chatbot + case-management. Auto-fills Chicago tenant ordinance notices.

### Workers & Wages
- **Wage Theft Calculator (EPI)** — Simple tool calculating stolen wages. $50B/year in U.S. wage theft (more than all robberies/burglaries combined). https://www.epi.org/publication/wage-theft-bigger-problem-forms-theft-workers/
- **ShedLight** — Workers upload pay stubs; AI extracts violations and generates demand letters.

### Immigration
- **Immigration Voice + AI Data Team** — Scraped USCIS data to identify processing outliers. 10,000+ cases for Writ of Mandamus filing. Win rate >90%.

### Policing
- **Open Policing Project (Stanford)** — 200M+ traffic-stop records. Found Black drivers 20% more likely to be searched. Used in class-action litigation.
- **Citizen Browser (The Markup)** — Paid panelists scraping Facebook algorithmic recommendations. Proved algorithm pushed inflammatory content more to Black users.

**Key Quote:** "FOIA is the recourse of the powerless. Structured data is the weapon of the organized." — Freddy Martinez, Lucy Parsons Labs.

## 4. AI LITERACY & GRASSROOTS

- **Algorithmic Justice League (AJL)** — Dr. Joy Buolamwini. Art + research + advocacy surfacing algorithmic bias. Free "Safe Face Pledge" toolkit. https://www.ajl.org
- **Data & Society Research Institute** — Free reports on algorithmic management, gig-work surveillance, tenant-screening AI. https://datasociety.net
- **Big Brother Watch (UK)** — "AI & Your Rights" workshops in public libraries. Teaches citizens to file Subject Access Requests. https://bigbrotherwatch.org.uk

**Key Statistic:** "Only 18% of U.S. adults can explain how machine learning works, but 73% encounter algorithmic systems daily." — Pew Research Center, 2023.

## 5. REAL CASE STUDIES

### Housing: "Worst Evictors" Database
- NYC's ANHD built eviction/violation databases. City Council passed disclosure law. Buildings converted to community land trusts.
- Source: ANHD Annual Report 2023; The City (NYC).

### Healthcare: TB Screening in Zambia
- Zebra Medical Vision deployed AI chest-X-ray in rural clinics with zero radiologists. 3x TB case detection. Treatment initiation from 3 weeks to 48 hours.
- Source: The Lancet Digital Health, 2021.

### Legal: DoNotPay & Parking Tickets
- Auto-generated appeals. 200,000+ tickets overturned, $4M+ saved.

### Labor: Amazon Algorithmic Management
- Workers logged "Time Off Task" firings. Data showed firings correlated with understaffed shifts, not laziness. California OSHA investigations; Warehouse Worker Protection Act signed (2022).

### Education: Khanmigo in Newark
- Title I middle schools pilot. 20% increase in math-assignment completion.

## 6. KEY STATISTICS & QUOTES

- **Justice Gap:** 92% of low-income Americans receive no legal help for civil issues — LSC, 2022.
- **Wage Theft:** $50B stolen from workers annually — EPI, 2014 (subsequent studies confirm).
- **Healthcare AI:** AI diagnostics in rural India achieved >90% dermatologist-level accuracy for 26 conditions at <$0.50/scan — Nature Medicine, 2021.
- **Digital Divide:** 34% of adults with income <$30K don't own a smartphone; 43% lack home broadband — Pew, 2021.
- **Algorithmic Bias:** Algorithm guiding decisions for 200M+ patients systematically underestimated Black patients' medical needs — Science, 2019.
- **Education:** Students using AI tutoring in low-resource settings show gains equivalent to 1.5–2 years of standard instruction — World Bank meta-analysis, 2023.
